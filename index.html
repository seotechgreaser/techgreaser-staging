Your website could be half-funded by your Local Enterprise Office

Small Irish businesses can apply for government co-funding for websites, e-commerce and digital marketing through their Local Enterprise Office (LEO). We'll scope your project, give you a quote broken down for the application and help you get the paperwork right.

Eligibility, amounts and co-funding rates are set by your LEO and can change. Apply and get approval before you sign any purchase order.

FAQ

Questions Irish business owners ask us

Can't see yours? Email info@techgreaser.com and a real person in Dublin will reply.

How much does digital marketing cost in Ireland?

It depends on your goals and competition. A focused local SEO plan for one location costs a lot less than a national Google Ads campaign. We quote fixed monthly fees in euro (plus VAT) after your free audit, and recommend ad budgets based on real cost-per-click data for your area.

How long does SEO take to work?

Google Business Profile and local fixes often show movement within weeks. Competitive national rankings usually take three to six months. We'll set honest expectations in your plan and report progress every month.

Do you only work with Dublin businesses?

No. Our office is in Dublin but we work with clients across Ireland, in Cork, Galway, Limerick, Waterford, Kilkenny and beyond, as well as overseas brands selling into the Irish market. Most of our work runs remotely, with in-person meetings when they help.

Can you help with the Trading Online Voucher application?

Yes. We'll provide a detailed, itemised quote for your LEO application and plan the project around the scheme's rules. You attend the LEO information session and submit the application, and we only begin once you have your letter of offer.

Is my website GDPR compliant?

Many Irish websites aren't. Common problems are cookie banners that load tracking before consent, missing privacy notices and contact forms with no consent wording. We include a compliance check in every audit and fix it as part of any build.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Ongoing services run month-to-month after a short onboarding period. Website builds are fixed-price projects with clear milestones.
